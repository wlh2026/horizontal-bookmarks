# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

**horizontal-bookmarks（横向收藏夹）扩展**

最后更新：2026-08-17

---

## 中文

本扩展**不收集、不传输、不存储任何个人数据到外部服务器**。

### 我们收集的数据
**无。** 本扩展不会将数据发送到任何远程服务器，也不会与任何第三方共享数据。

### 本扩展使用的浏览器权限及其用途
| 权限 | 用途 | 是否离开本机 |
|------|------|--------------|
| `bookmarks` | 读取您的书签树以在面板中展示，并打开您点击的书签 | 否 |
| `windows` | 以独立窗口打开书签面板，并记忆窗口大小/最大化状态 | 否 |
| `storage` | 仅将您的界面设置（列宽、行高、列数、字号）与窗口尺寸保存在**本地浏览器** | 否 |

### 数据存储位置
所有设置与窗口状态仅保存在您本地浏览器的 `chrome.storage.local` 中，不会上传。

### 您的权利
由于我们不持有任何您的个人数据，因此无需提供数据导出或删除流程。如您卸载本扩展，相关数据会随浏览器本地存储一并清除。

### 联系方式
如对本隐私政策有疑问，请通过项目仓库 Issues 联系：
https://github.com/wlh2026/horizontal-bookmarks/issues

## English

This extension **does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data to any external server.**

### Data we collect
**None.** This extension does not send any data to remote servers and does not share data with any third party.

### Permissions used and why
| Permission | Purpose | Leaves the device? |
|------------|---------|--------------------|
| `bookmarks` | Read your bookmark tree to display it in the panel, and open the bookmark you click | No |
| `windows` | Open the bookmarks panel in its own window and remember its size / maximized state | No |
| `storage` | Store only your UI preferences (column width, row height, column count, font size) and window size in the **local browser** | No |

### Where data is stored
All settings and window state are stored only in your local browser via `chrome.storage.local` and are never uploaded.

### Your rights
Because we hold no personal data about you, no data-export or deletion process is needed. Uninstalling the extension removes the related local data.

### Contact
Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to the project Issues page:
https://github.com/wlh2026/horizontal-bookmarks/issues
